About Us

Chartered Accountants, Chartered Tax Practitioners & Financial Consultants

Omaco Professional Services is a firm of Chartered Accountants, Chartered Tax Practitioners, Training, Financial and Management Consultants. The firm was established to provide a wide range of business, financial, tax, consultancy, training and advisory services to small, medium and large scale business organizations.

Both the partners and the staff have varying degrees of experience in governmental and non-governmental operations. With the development of information technology, the firm has established its own Computer Division which offers support services to other departments.

Our Goals

What We Want To Achieve

Professional Excellence

To provide accounting, auditing, tax management and other allied services at the highest level of professionalism at all times, comparable with what is obtainable either here in Nigeria or elsewhere.

Integrity First

To maintain the highest integrity in the discharge of our duties as professionals towards our clients and the society in which we operate.

Youth Development

To train young people who are interested in this profession, inculcating in them the entire attributes relating to professionalism and integrity.

Sustainable Growth

To make a fair profit thus justifying our existence as a going concern, while continuing to invest in the development of our people and services.
